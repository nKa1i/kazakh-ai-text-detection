\section{Methodology}
\label{sec:method}

We formulate fact verification as an end-to-end framework consisting of three coordinated stages (Figure~\ref{fig:framework_architecture}): (1) Morphologically-Grounded Hybrid Evidence Retrieval, (2) Morphological NLI Verification, and (3) Joint Credibility Calibration via the 2D Trust Matrix.

\subsection{Hybrid Evidence Retrieval}
Given query claim $c$ and evidence corpus $\mathcal{E} = \{e_1, \dots, e_N\}$ ($N \approx 250,000$), standard BM25 fails in Kazakh due to morphological mismatch between inflected word forms, while dense retrievers often miss exact entity references.

We resolve this by combining a morpheme-stemmed sparse retriever with a multilingual dense encoder. Each claim $c$ and passage $e$ is processed by a Kazakh morphological stemmer that strips inflectional suffixes while retaining grammatical roots, yielding $c_{\text{morph}}$ and $e_{\text{morph}}$. The BM25 score ($k_1 = 1.5, b = 0.75$) is computed over these stemmed representations:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:bm25}
\begin{split}
S_{\text{BM25}}&(c_{\text{morph}}, e_{\text{morph}}) = \sum_{t \in c_{\text{morph}}} \mathrm{IDF}(t) \\
&\times \frac{f(t, e_{\text{morph}}) \cdot (k_1 + 1)}{f(t, e_{\text{morph}}) + k_1 \left(1 - b + b \cdot \frac{|e_{\text{morph}}|}{\mathrm{avgdl}}\right)}
\end{split}
\end{equation}
Concurrently, we encode unstemmed surface texts into dense vectors $\mathbf{h}_c = \mathrm{Encoder}(c)$ and $\mathbf{h}_e = \mathrm{Encoder}(e) \in \mathbb{R}^d$ using \textsc{mContriever}~\citep{izacard2022mcontriever}. The hybrid evidence score balances normalized sparse scoring and dense cosine similarity ($\alpha = 0.45$):
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:hybrid_retrieval}
\begin{split}
S_{\text{hybrid}}(c, e) &= \alpha \cdot S_{\text{BM25}}(c_{\text{morph}}, e_{\text{morph}}) \\
&\quad + (1 - \alpha) \cdot \cos\left(\mathbf{h}_c, \mathbf{h}_e\right)
\end{split}
\end{equation}
Alternatively, for large candidate pools, Reciprocal Rank Fusion~\citep{mudunuri-etal-2026-bounded} aggregates rankings: $\mathrm{RRF}(c, e) = \sum_{m} (k_0 + r_m(c, e))^{-1}$ with $k_0 = 60$. The top-$k$ retrieved passages form candidate evidence set $E = \{e_{(1)}, \dots, e_{(k)}\}$.

\subsection{Morphological NLI Verifier}
Given claim $c$ and concatenated evidence passage $E$, the verification module predicts veracity label $y \in \{\textsc{Supported}, \textsc{Refuted}, \textsc{Not Enough Info}\}$.

Standard cross-encoders process sequence $\texttt{[CLS]} \circ c \circ \texttt{[SEP]} \circ E \circ \texttt{[SEP]}$ through a pretrained transformer to extract pooled representation $\mathbf{h}_{[\text{CLS}]} \in \mathbb{R}^d$. Because standard subword tokenizers fragment agglutinative Turkic morphology into arbitrary byte sequences, we extract an explicit 16-dimensional morphological diagnostic vector $\mathbf{m}_{\text{affix}} \in \mathbb{R}^{16}$.

\paragraph{16-Dimensional Diagnostic Extractor}
The vector $\mathbf{m}_{\text{affix}} = [m_0, \dots, m_{15}]^{\top} \in [0, 1]^{16}$ captures fine-grained alignments across five functional tiers:
(1)~\textbf{Polarity Alignment ($m_0, m_1, m_2$)}: Indicators $m_0 = \mathbb{I}(c \text{ has neg})$ and $m_1 = \mathbb{I}(E \text{ has neg})$ detect verbal negation allomorphs (\textit{-ба/-бе/-па/-пе/-ма/-ме}, \textit{-маған/-меген}, \textit{-майды/-мейді}) and copular particles (\textit{емес, жоқ}), with $m_2 = \Delta_{\text{neg}} = |m_0 - m_1|$ capturing directional negation mismatch (XOR).
(2)~\textbf{Temporal/Numerical Conflicts ($m_3, m_4$)}: $m_3 = \Delta_{\text{year}} \in \{0, 1\}$ detects calendar year conflicts when 4-digit year sets are disjoint ($\mathcal{Y}_c \cap \mathcal{Y}_e = \emptyset$); $m_4 = \Delta_{\text{num}} \in \{0, 1\}$ flags quantitative mismatches where claim numbers are absent from $E$.
(3)~\textbf{Evidentiality and Modality ($m_5, \dots, m_8$)}: $m_5, m_6$ detect indirect evidential markers (\textit{-ыпты/-іпті}, \textit{екен}) in $c$ and $E$, distinguishing non-witnessed hearsay from witnessed past assertions (\textit{-ды/-ді}); $m_7, m_8$ identify necessity (\textit{керек, тиіс, қажет}) and possibility operators (\textit{мүмкін, ықтимал}).
(4)~\textbf{FST Root vs.\ Surface Overlap ($m_9, \dots, m_{11}$)}: Using our 83-rule FST analyzer, canonical root sets $\mathcal{R}_c, \mathcal{R}_e$ and surface word sets $\mathcal{S}_c, \mathcal{S}_e$ yield FST root Jaccard $m_9 = J_{\text{root}} = \frac{|\mathcal{R}_c \cap \mathcal{R}_e|}{|\mathcal{R}_c \cup \mathcal{R}_e|}$, surface overlap $m_{10} = J_{\text{surf}} = \frac{|\mathcal{S}_c \cap \mathcal{S}_e|}{|\mathcal{S}_c \cup \mathcal{S}_e|}$, and inflectional divergence $m_{11} = \Delta_{\text{div}} = |J_{\text{surf}} - J_{\text{root}}|$.
(5)~\textbf{Syntactic Agreement and Length Scaling ($m_{12}, \dots, m_{15}$)}: $m_{12} = s_{\text{match}} \in \{0, 1\}$ indicates proper-noun subject grounding; $m_{13} = J_{\text{case}}$ measures root agreement across nominal case inflections (genitive, dative, accusative, locative, ablative); $m_{14} = \min(1.0, |c| / 30)$ and $m_{15} = \min(1.0, |E| / 100)$ normalize sequence lengths.

\paragraph{Fused Classification and Training Objective}
Contextual embedding $\mathbf{h}_{[\text{CLS}]} \in \mathbb{R}^d$ and affix vector $\mathbf{m}_{\text{affix}} \in \mathbb{R}^{16}$ are projected to class logits:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:nli_verifier}
P(y \mid c, E) = \mathrm{softmax}\left(\mathbf{W}_v \left[\mathbf{h}_{[\text{CLS}]}; \mathbf{m}_{\text{affix}}\right] + \mathbf{b}_v\right)
\end{equation}
where $\mathbf{W}_v \in \mathbb{R}^{3 \times (d + 16)}$ and $\mathbf{b}_v \in \mathbb{R}^3$. To counteract class imbalance ($N = 638$; $N_{\textsc{Sup}} = 274, N_{\textsc{Ref}} = 154, N_{\textsc{NEI}} = 210$), we optimize class-weighted cross-entropy:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:class_weighted_loss}
\mathcal{L}_{\text{NLI}} = - \sum_{k=1}^3 w_k \cdot y_k \log P(y = k \mid c, E)
\end{equation}
with inverse-frequency weights $w_k = 3 \cdot \frac{N_k^{-1}}{\sum_{j=1}^3 N_j^{-1}}$ ($w_{\textsc{Sup}} = 0.735, w_{\textsc{Ref}} = 1.307, w_{\textsc{NEI}} = 0.958$), penalizing false confidence on Hard NEI.

Optimization uses AdamW ($\beta_1 = 0.9, \beta_2 = 0.999$, weight decay $0.01$) with differential learning rates ($\text{lr}_{\text{encoder}} = 2 \times 10^{-5}, \text{lr}_{\text{head}} = 2 \times 10^{-4}$) and a cosine annealing schedule over total steps $T_{\text{total}}$ with warmup $T_{\text{warm}} = 0.1 \cdot T_{\text{total}}$: $\eta(t) = \eta_{\text{base}} \frac{t}{T_{\text{warm}}}$ for $t < T_{\text{warm}}$, and $\frac{\eta_{\text{base}}}{2} [1 + \cos(\frac{t - T_{\text{warm}}}{0.9 \cdot T_{\text{total}}} \pi)]$ for $t \ge T_{\text{warm}}$.

\subsection{2D Trust Matrix Formulation}
To account for factual truth and generative provenance simultaneously, we map predictions into a continuous 2D trust space $\mathcal{T} = [-1, 1] \times [0, 1]$.

We define the \textbf{Factual Veracity Score} $T_{\text{fact}} \in [-1, 1]$ as the calibrated difference between entailment and contradiction probabilities:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:trust_fact}
T_{\text{fact}} = P(\textsc{Supported} \mid c, E) - P(\textsc{Refuted} \mid c, E)
\end{equation}
where $T_{\text{fact}} > 0$ indicates verified support, $T_{\text{fact}} < 0$ signifies refutation, and $T_{\text{fact}} \approx 0$ corresponds to insufficient evidence.

The \textbf{Generative Authenticity Score} $T_{\text{gen}} \in [0, 1]$ reflects human-authorship probability:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:trust_gen}
T_{\text{gen}} = 1 - P(\text{AI-Generated} \mid c)
\end{equation}
estimated via a calibrated classifier trained on parallel human-machine Kazakh corpora.

The unified scalar \textbf{Trust Metric} $\mathcal{T}(x)$ integrates both dimensions:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:trust_score}
\mathcal{T}(x) = \sqrt{\frac{1}{2} \left(\max(0, T_{\text{fact}})^2 + T_{\text{gen}}^2\right)}
\end{equation}
inducing four operational quadrants: (I)~\textit{Verified Human Ground Truth} ($T_{\text{fact}} > 0, T_{\text{gen}} \ge 0.5$); (II)~\textit{Human Misinformation} ($T_{\text{fact}} \le 0, T_{\text{gen}} \ge 0.5$); (III)~\textit{Malicious AI Hallucination} ($T_{\text{fact}} \le 0, T_{\text{gen}} < 0.5$); and (IV)~\textit{Factual AI Synthesis} ($T_{\text{fact}} > 0, T_{\text{gen}} < 0.5$).
