\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

In this paper, we presented \textbf{Kazakh-FEVER 3K}, the first morphologically-grounded fact verification benchmark for Kazakh, comprising 3,000 human-validated claim-evidence pairs. Our Hard NEI protocol targets fine-grained morphological reasoning over negation, evidentials, and modality. To address agglutinative vocabulary sparsity, our hybrid retrieval pipeline couples morpheme-stemmed BM25 with dense representations via Reciprocal Rank Fusion, achieving 99.8\% Recall@5 over 250,000 passages. Furthermore, our morphological NLI verifier combines contextual representations with 16-D affix features and a 2D Trust Matrix evaluating veracity and generative provenance, reaching 82.6\% accuracy and 82.1\% Macro-F1 (+10.8\% over XLM-RoBERTa-base and +11.5\% on Hard NEI). Future work will extend this framework across related Turkic languages and multimodal evidence streams.
