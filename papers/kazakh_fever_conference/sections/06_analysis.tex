\section{Linguistic Analysis and Case Studies}
\label{sec:analysis}

\subsection{Error Analysis across Kazakh Affixation Chains}
To understand the linguistic failure modes of standard models, we analyze prediction errors across five canonical categories of Kazakh morphological constructions:

\paragraph{1. Negation Suffix Interference}
In Kazakh, verbal negation is expressed through the suffix allomorphs \textit{-ба/-бе/-па/-пе/-ма/-ме}, placed directly after the verbal root and preceding tense and person suffixes. For instance, the distinction between \textit{барған} (went) and \textit{бармаған} (did not go) is mediated by the single morpheme \textit{-ма-}. Because subword tokenizers frequently partition \textit{бармаған} into subwords that blur this boundary, pretrained encoders such as mBERT and XLM-RoBERTa frequently misclassify negative claims as \textsc{Supported} when all surrounding nominal arguments match the evidence text. Across 120 negated test instances, baseline XLM-RoBERTa exhibited an error rate of 38.3\%, whereas our morphology-aware verifier reduced this error rate to 12.5\%.

\paragraph{2. Indirect Evidentiality and Epistemic Modality}
Kazakh grammar explicitly distinguishes between directly witnessed past events (marked by \textit{-ды/-ді}) and indirect, reportative, or inferential events (marked by \textit{-ыпты/-іпті} or the particle \textit{екен}). In investigative journalism and encyclopedic texts, claims asserting direct factual certainty when the source passage expresses speculative or hearsay modality should be classified as \textsc{Not Enough Info}. Standard models universally fail to distinguish these nuances, treating evidential markers as stylistic synonyms rather than truth-conditional operators.

\paragraph{3. Case-Agreement and Syntactic Role Inversion}
Kazakh is an SOV (Subject-Object-Verb) language where grammatical relations are marked by accusative (\textit{-ны/-ні}), dative (\textit{-ға/-ге}), locative (\textit{-да/-де}), and ablative (\textit{-дан/-ден}) suffixes. When claims invert agent-patient roles while retaining the identical set of lexical stems, bag-of-words and naive dense encoders fail to identify the contradiction, whereas our model preserves case alignments through explicit morphological feature encoding.

\subsection{Interactive Explainability and Production Deployment}
\label{sec:explainability}

To facilitate transparent auditing, operational deployment, and human-in-the-loop fact-checking for agglutinative low-resource languages, we implement and deploy an interactive demonstration system publicly available on Hugging Face Spaces at \url{https://huggingface.co/spaces/nKa1i/kazakh-ai-text-detector}. Developed with the Gradio web framework, the system provides sub-second document and claim-level verification accessible via modern web browsers, featuring comprehensive bilingual localization in both Kazakh (\textit{қазақ тілі}) and English across all interface components, telemetry panels, and diagnostic views.

\paragraph{Continuous 2D Trust Matrix Cartesian Plane}
Rather than reducing credibility to an isolated binary or ternary classification label, the web interface visually maps each submitted document into the continuous two-dimensional trust space $\mathcal{T} = [-1, 1] \times [0, 1]$ formalized in Section~\ref{sec:method}. The system generates a publication-grade, procedural SVG rendering of the Cartesian plane in real time directly within the client browser, completely eliminating external graphics rendering overhead.

The horizontal axis evaluates factual veracity $T_{\text{fact}} \in [-1, 1]$ (Equation~\ref{eq:trust_fact}), spanning from completely refuted ($T_{\text{fact}} = -1.0$) through ungrounded neutral evidence ($T_{\text{fact}} = 0.0$) to fully supported ground truth ($T_{\text{fact}} = +1.0$). The vertical axis measures generative authenticity $T_{\text{gen}} \in [0, 1]$ (Equation~\ref{eq:trust_gen}), demarcating machine-synthesized text ($T_{\text{gen}} = 0.0$) from human-authored text ($T_{\text{gen}} = 1.0$). Orthogonal crosshairs centered at $(T_{\text{fact}} = 0.0, T_{\text{gen}} = 0.5)$ partition the Cartesian plane into four pastel-coded operational risk quadrants:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Quadrant I ($T_{\text{fact}} > 0, T_{\text{gen}} \ge 0.5$)}: \textit{Verified Human Ground Truth} (emerald green), denoting authentic, factually corroborated human discourse.
    \item \textbf{Quadrant II ($T_{\text{fact}} \le 0, T_{\text{gen}} \ge 0.5$)}: \textit{Human Misinformation} (rose red), capturing erroneous, unsubstantiated, or misleading assertions produced by human authors.
    \item \textbf{Quadrant III ($T_{\text{fact}} \le 0, T_{\text{gen}} < 0.5$)}: \textit{Malicious AI Hallucination} (amber gold), identifying deceptive, ungrounded synthetic text generated by large language models.
    \item \textbf{Quadrant IV ($T_{\text{fact}} > 0, T_{\text{gen}} < 0.5$)}: \textit{Factual AI Synthesis} (sky blue), representing factually accurate information synthesized by generative AI systems.
\end{itemize}
An active indicator rendered with an animated pulsing ring highlights the document's continuous coordinate $(c_x, c_y) = (200 + 160 \cdot T_{\text{fact}}, 260 - 200 \cdot T_{\text{gen}})$, bounded cleanly within the visual display canvas. Above the plane, an active Heads-Up Display (HUD) telemetry bar presents exact real-time metrics for $T_{\text{fact}}$, $T_{\text{gen}}$, the assigned quadrant designation, and the unified composite trust metric:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{T}(x) = \sqrt{\frac{1}{2} \left(\max(0, T_{\text{fact}})^2 + T_{\text{gen}}^2\right)}
\end{equation}
providing journalists and fact-checkers with a single, calibrated scalar confidence metric bounded in $[0, 1]$.

\paragraph{Dual-Model Comparison Toggle}
To demonstrate the concrete utility of morphological feature grounding in real time, the interface incorporates a runtime model toggle between \textit{Ours (Hybrid + Morpho)} and \textit{Baseline (Surface Only)}. In baseline mode, the verification pipeline relies solely on standard subword cross-encoding without morphological feature fusion. In side-by-side evaluations, users can observe how subtle suffix manipulations (such as the insertion of the negation allomorph \textit{-ба-} or epistemic speculation \textit{мүмкін}) confound the baseline model due to high lexical entity overlap, whereas our proposed system correctly flags contradictory or ungrounded claims.

\paragraph{16-Feature Morphological Diagnostic Drawers and Stem Highlighting}
Underneath the document-level executive trust card, the system extracts and displays every atomic claim in a structured verification table detailing predicted verdicts (\textsc{Supported}, \textsc{Refuted}, or \textsc{Not Enough Info}), model confidence percentages, top-ranked evidence citations, and concise natural language explanations.

To provide complete interpretability for each atomic verification decision, each table row integrates an expandable diagnostic drawer (implemented via collapsible HTML5 elements). When expanded by an analyst, this drawer exposes the complete 16-dimensional morphological diagnostic vector $\mathbf{m}_{\text{affix}} \in \mathbb{R}^{16}$ formalized in Section~\ref{sec:method}:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Directional Negation Mismatch ($\Delta_{\text{neg}}$)}: Displays a color-coded status badge indicating whether a verbal negation allomorph (\textit{-ба/-бе/-па/-пе/-ма/-ме}, past \textit{-маған/-меген}) or negative copula (\textit{емес, жоқ}) is present in the claim but absent in the evidence (or vice versa), triggering instant contradiction warnings.
    \item \textbf{Temporal and Numerical Conflict Indicators}: Flags calendar year discrepancies ($\Delta_{\text{year}}$) and numerical quantity mismatches ($\Delta_{\text{num}}$) whenever quantitative assertions deviate from source documentation.
    \item \textbf{Evidential and Epistemic Modality Badges}: Identifies indirect inferential markers (\textit{-ыпты/-іпті}, \textit{екен}) and epistemic modal operators (\textit{керек, тиіс, мүмкін, ықтимал}), alerting auditors to hypothetical speculation or non-witnessed claims indicative of Hard NEI instances.
    \item \textbf{FST Root Jaccard vs.\ Surface Token Overlap}: Visualizes normalized mini-bar progress tracks comparing finite-state morphological root similarity ($J_{\text{root}}$) against raw surface token overlap ($J_{\text{surf}}$), quantifying inflectional divergence ($\Delta_{\text{div}}$) that causes standard subword tokenizers to fail.
    \item \textbf{Case Suffix and Grammatical Alignment}: Reports percentage alignment across nominal case inflection chains (accusative, dative, locative, ablative) and verifies proper noun subject grounding.
\end{enumerate}
Crucially, within the top-retrieved evidence passage box embedded inside each diagnostic drawer, the interface applies real-time matched stem highlighting: all lexical roots shared between the claim and the retrieved evidence passage (as identified by our 83-rule FST analyzer) are rendered with distinctive visual highlighting. This provides immediate visual transparency, enabling human fact-checkers to inspect the exact morphemic alignments that justify the model's factual verdict and dramatically reducing verification latency during high-stakes information auditing.
